My first app : AI-Powered Investment Research Assistant
Problem Statement

Retail investors and financial advisors spend significant time gathering information from multiple sources before making investment decisions. They must analyze stock fundamentals, analyst recommendations, and market news, which can be overwhelming and time-consuming.

As a result, investors may:
Miss important market-moving news.
Make decisions based on incomplete information.
Struggle to track multiple stocks efficiently.
React too slowly to changing market conditions.
Proposed Solution

Develop a Multi-Agent Financial Research Assistant that automates the investment research process.
